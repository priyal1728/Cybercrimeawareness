Main Cybercrime Awareness Site

This website is designed to help users recognize common cybercrimes such as phishing, online fraud, identity theft, and cyberbullying.

  • Understand how cybercriminals use fake messages, scams, and websites to steal information.
  • Learn the warning signs of online fraud, phishing, fake accounts, and suspicious links.
  • Protect accounts using strong passwords, two-factor authentication, and safe browsing habits.
  • Report unsafe activity, save evidence, and seek help from a trusted person when needed.
